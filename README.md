# Greenhouse machinery selection checklist

For growers planning a polyhouse or greenhouse who need to decide which machinery goes inside it, and in what order.

## Climate
- Curtain gear and roof vent motors for natural ventilation.
- Fogging or misting where cooling is needed: [misting and fogging systems](https://www.blustal.com/products/misting-fogging-systems/).

## Water and nutrition
- Filtration, pump and fertigation unit sized for the whole area.
- Smart control if you want scheduling by sensor: [smart irrigation](https://www.blustal.com/products/smart-irrigation/).

## Structure and fixing
- Structure kit for the crop and the wind zone: [greenhouse structure kits](https://www.blustal.com/products/greenhouse-structure-kits/).
- Film locking, spring wire, clamps and turn buckles: [greenhouse fixing](https://www.blustal.com/products/greenhouse-fixing/).
